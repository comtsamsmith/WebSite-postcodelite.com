Integration

Do you provide a Web service I can call to get data

Yes, click here to see our HTTP/XML Web Service

Do you provide sample code?

Yes, we have much sample code in Visual Basic, ASP.NET, VB.NET and PHP.  Please see either the Programmers developers toolkit, Premises Web Integration, Street Web Integration or HTTP/XML Web Service. 

Can the Desktop Software interact with common applications?

Our Postcode Lite Desktop software allows you to lookup addresses and simply copy the address to the clipboard.  We also have a "Professional Version" which detects the current application you are using, so after finding the address it will pop the address in the appropriate fields in your favourite application.

Selling to your customers

Can I be a reseller?

Sorry we do not offer any reseller margin because of our low prices.  We do however have the facility to create an account to oversee/manage customer accounts.

I am a developer, but how do I create an account for a customer

Each of your customers, MUST have a separate account set-up, and credits purchased for each customer.   YOU must make sure that the end customer has understood our terms

Purchasing License/Credits

How do I pay?

 You can purchase licenses on-line and pay by PayPal or Google checkout.

Do I have to wait for License?

As soon as the license is paid for it becomes active for internet based data.  To use local data files, e-mail your License ID and Site Code taken from the "Manager.exe" unlock program.  Contact us

Data related

Do you send me a CD-ROM?

To keep our prices low, we only offer downloads.  Indeed this means you can download, open an account, trial, then purchase and use within minutes.

Can the credit packs be used within my organisation on our Intranet?

The Royal Mail Terms and Conditions specify that for use within an organisation, by an employee, using any interface, that a license must be purchased for each user.  This includes use within your Intranet or web application.

Can a User License be used on my public web site?

The Royal Mail Terms and Conditions specify that for use on a public web site, you must purchase credit packs and pay per address search.

How often is the data updated?

Our internet data is updated every month.  Local data files are updates every four months, we send you an e-mail when an update is ready for download.

I have local data files, how do I get updates?

We e-mail you when updates are available, which can be downloaded, and copied to your Network or PC.
